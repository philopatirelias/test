# General Practice Interview Template
## Opening questions
- What brings you in today?
## Symptom characterization
- Onset, duration, severity, context, triggers.
## Associated symptoms
- Review relevant system symptoms and danger signs.
## Medication/allergy questions
- Current meds, adherence, allergies.
## Lifestyle questions
- Diet, exercise, smoking, alcohol, sleep, stress, caffeine.
## Red flags
- Safety-relevant missing questions for severe or progressive symptoms.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- Which high-risk details are still unclear?
