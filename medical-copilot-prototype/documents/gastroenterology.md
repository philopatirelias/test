# Gastroenterology Interview Template
## Opening questions
- What abdominal or digestive symptoms are most concerning?
## Symptom characterization
- Pain location, severity, relation to meals, bowel changes.
## Associated symptoms
- Nausea, vomiting, bleeding signs, fever, weight loss.
## Medication/allergy questions
- NSAID use, current meds, allergies.
## Lifestyle questions
- Diet patterns, alcohol use, smoking.
## Red flags
- Severe pain, GI bleeding, fever/systemic illness, pregnancy relevance.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- What dangerous causes still need clarification?
