# Neurology Interview Template
## Opening questions
- Describe the neurological symptom and onset.
## Symptom characterization
- Onset speed, progression, severity, triggers.
## Associated symptoms
- Weakness, speech changes, visual changes, confusion, fever/neck stiffness.
## Medication/allergy questions
- Anticoagulants, immunosuppressants, medication allergies.
## Lifestyle questions
- Sleep, stress, caffeine, substance use.
## Red flags
- Neurological deficits, thunderclap onset, syncope, systemic illness.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- Which urgent deficits still need clarification?
