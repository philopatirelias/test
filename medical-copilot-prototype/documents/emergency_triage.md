# Emergency Triage Interview Template (Interview Support)
## Opening questions
- What happened and when did it start?
## Symptom characterization
- Severity trajectory, sudden onset, functional impact.
## Associated symptoms
- Dyspnea, syncope, neuro deficits, bleeding, fever.
## Medication/allergy questions
- Allergies, anticoagulants, immunosuppression.
## Lifestyle questions
- Relevant recent exposures and substance triggers.
## Red flags
- Any immediate safety-relevant missing question.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- Clarify urgent risk indicators; do not assume diagnosis.
