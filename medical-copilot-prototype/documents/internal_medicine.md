# Internal Medicine Interview Template
## Opening questions
- What is your main concern today?
- When did symptoms begin and how have they changed?
## Symptom characterization
- Location, severity, timing, triggers, relieving factors.
## Associated symptoms
- Shortness of breath, fever, syncope, neurological symptoms.
## Medication/allergy questions
- Current medicines, medication adherence, allergies.
## Lifestyle questions
- Sleep, diet, stress, smoking, alcohol, caffeine.
## Red flags
- Safety-relevant missing questions: exertional symptoms, syncope, severe pain, neurological deficits.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- What important urgent context still needs clarification?
