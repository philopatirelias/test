# Cardiology Interview Template
## Opening questions
- Describe chest discomfort or palpitations in your own words.
## Symptom characterization
- Exertional relationship, duration, radiation, associated dyspnea.
## Associated symptoms
- Syncope, diaphoresis, nausea, exercise intolerance.
## Medication/allergy questions
- Cardiac meds, anticoagulants, allergies.
## Lifestyle questions
- Caffeine, stimulants, alcohol, stress, sleep.
## Red flags
- Exertional pain, shortness of breath, syncope, anticoagulant use.
## Safety-relevant missing questions
- Any safety-relevant missing question that could change urgency?
